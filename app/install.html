Install the app

Pick your device and follow the steps, it takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Get the Downloader app

    On the Fire TV home screen, go to the magnifying glass (Find), choose Search and type Downloader.

    Pick the app with the orange icon, then press Get or Download and wait for it to finish.

  2. Open Downloader

    Press Open. If it asks to allow access to your photos, media and files, press Allow. Press OK on any welcome messages.

  3. Type the address

    Click the box at the top, and type this exactly (no spaces, all small letters):

    __ADDR__

    Then press Go.

    The letters are hard to type

    Use the on-screen keyboard with your remote's arrow buttons and OK. Or install the free Amazon Fire TV app on your phone, which lets you type on the phone instead.

  4. Wait, then press Install

    It downloads by itself. When it shows Install at the bottom right, press Install.

    It says "can't install" or "unknown apps"

    This is normal. Press Settings, turn Downloader to On, then press the Back button on your remote and press Install again.

    If there is no Settings button: on the Fire TV home screen go to Settings, then My Fire TV, then Developer options, then Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on. If you do not see Developer options, go to About and press OK on the device name 7 times.

  5. Open it

    When it says the app is installed, press Open.

    If Downloader asks about deleting the file, press Delete and then Delete again, you don't need it any more.

That's it!

The app is on your TV now. You can find it with your other apps.

    Scroll all the way to the bottom of the list. If it is still not there, tap Edit Actions at the very bottom and add it, or tap View More if you see that.

    Tap Add at the top right. The app's icon is on your Home Screen now.

    Tap the icon on your Home Screen. It opens full screen, just like an app.
